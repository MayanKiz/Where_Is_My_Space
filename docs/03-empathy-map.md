# User Empathy Map

## Target user

A person looking for an available or suitable space in a crowded physical location.

## Think & Feel

- “Is there any space available?”
- “Will I actually find a suitable place?”
- “Can my group sit together?”
- “Is there a less-crowded area?”
- “I do not want to waste time checking multiple places.”

## See

- Crowded areas
- People occupying spaces for long periods
- Multiple rooms, floors, or zones
- No clear real-time availability information
- Existing cameras that do not provide user-facing occupancy intelligence

## Hear

- “That place is full.”
- “Try the other area.”
- “Go and check first.”
- “Can we find a place for four people?”
- “Maybe someone will leave soon.”

## Say & Do

- Walks around searching
- Checks multiple locations
- Asks friends or staff
- Waits or changes location
- Manually compares available options

## Pains

- No real-time visibility
- Wasted time
- Uncertainty before reaching a location
- Difficult group-space discovery
- Unpredictable crowd levels

## Gains

- Real-time availability
- Suitable-space recommendations
- Group-space discovery
- Less-crowded options
- Notifications when space becomes available
- Better use of existing facilities

## Key insight

> **Users do not need more cameras. They need useful intelligence from the infrastructure that already exists.**
