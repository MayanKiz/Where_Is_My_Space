# Product Vision

## Vision

Make physical spaces as discoverable and understandable as digital spaces.

Today, people often know that a location exists but do not know whether it is useful at the moment they need it. WIMS aims to provide a real-time intelligence layer that explains how spaces are being used.

## Mission

Convert existing authorized infrastructure into actionable information that helps people find the right space with less uncertainty and less wasted time.

## Product principle

> **People should not have to physically search for availability when the environment can communicate it intelligently.**

## Long-term vision

WIMS can evolve from a campus-focused prototype into a general spatial intelligence platform for:

- Education
- Healthcare
- Workplaces
- Events
- Public facilities
- Transportation
- Hospitality
- Co-working
- Waiting and service environments

## What WIMS is not

WIMS is not simply a CCTV viewer, a people counter, or a seat-booking application.

It is a **spatial intelligence layer** that converts observations about physical occupancy into useful decisions.

## Success definition

A successful first version should answer three questions reliably:

1. Where is space available?
2. How crowded is it?
3. Which available space best matches the user's needs?
